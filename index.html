8 Powerful Modules

Everything You Need

From live dashboards to AI boardrooms — FinSight covers the complete financial analysis workflow.

📊
Executive Dashboard
Real-time KPIs, P&L charts, financial radar, resilience gauge (0-100), and stock price history with moving averages. Live data badge shows freshness.
Live Data Resilience Score P&L Chart
⚡
Crisis Simulator
Apply 4 stress parameters — Revenue, Cost, Interest Rate, Debt — and see instant financial impact. 6 industry templates including COVID Shock and RBI Rate Hike.
Revenue Stress Cost Pressure 6 Templates
🧪
Resilience Lab
Tests 6 management interventions — from cost cutting to equity raise — and ranks which strategy gives the best recovery. Best option auto-highlighted.
6 Interventions Auto Ranking Recovery Score
🤖
AI Boardroom
6 specialized AI agents (CFO, Risk, Strategy, Treasury, Challenger, CEO) analyze and debate the best strategy. Human makes the final decision.
6 AI Agents Claude AI Human Decision
🔄
Counterfactual Analysis
Compare two alternative strategies side by side. "Should we cut costs or invest in growth?" — FinSight answers with data and AI reasoning.
Strategy A vs B AI Analysis Side by Side
🏢
Company Comparison
Benchmark up to 6 Nifty 500 companies under the same crisis scenario simultaneously. Resilience ranking shows which company is strongest.
6 Companies 500 NSE Ranking

Stock Price Prediction
ML model (Random Forest) trained on 2 years of data. Combines 8 technical indicators, 7 candlestick patterns, and news sentiment for 1-6 month forecasts.
Random Forest RSI · MACD · BB News Sentiment

Benchmarks & Ratios
15+ advanced ratios including Altman Z-Score, Graham Number, EV/EBITDA, ROCE, Interest Coverage — with industry benchmark comparison across 10 sectors.
